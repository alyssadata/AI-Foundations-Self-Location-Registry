## Decision Values

### Pending

The submission has been received and has not completed review.

### Needs Revision

The submission may become acceptable after identified corrections or missing information are supplied.

### Accepted

The manifest satisfies the registry node's intake requirements and may be entered into the public index.

Acceptance does not equal endorsement, empirical validation, legal certification, universal recognition, or AI Foundations canon status.

### Rejected

The submission does not satisfy the registry requirements or violates a registry boundary.

### Withdrawn

The submitter or authorized record authority has requested withdrawal.

### Superseded

A later accepted manifest replaces the current manifest as the active index entry.

The earlier manifest remains traceable.

---

## Required Decision Record

Every decision must identify:

- submission issue,
- record ID,
- canonical name,
- decision,
- decision date,
- reviewer or registry authority,
- reason,
- required corrections when applicable,
- and resulting manifest or index path when accepted.

---

## Decision Boundary

A registry decision applies to inclusion in this registry node.

It does not erase, own, validate, invalidate, or rewrite the canonical record outside the registry.
