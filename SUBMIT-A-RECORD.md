# Submit a Record

Use this process to request inclusion in the public Self-Location Registry.

## Before Submitting

Confirm that:

- a complete Self-Location Record exists,
- the record is published at a stable canonical location,
- you are the record holder or are authorized to submit it,
- the public fields may be indexed,
- the canonical record states its source and authority,
- and the requested entry does not expose private information.

## Submission Steps

1. Copy `INDEX-MANIFEST-TEMPLATE.yaml`.
2. Complete every required field.
3. Confirm that the canonical record URL is publicly reachable.
4. Prepare evidence of your authority or delegated authority.
5. Open a new **Index Manifest Submission** issue.
6. Paste the completed manifest into the issue form.
7. Respond to requests for clarification or correction.
8. Wait for a recorded registry decision.

## Public Information Warning

This repository is public.

Do not submit private addresses, private contact information, government identifiers, credentials, confidential records, or information you do not have authority to publish.

## Required Permission Statement

> I am authorized to submit this Index Manifest. I authorize this registry node to validate the public source, preserve the submitted manifest and its version, create a searchable index entry, and point users and systems back to the canonical record under the permissions stated in the manifest.

This limited indexing authorization does not transfer authorship, ownership, or authority over the canonical record.
