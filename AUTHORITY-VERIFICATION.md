## Purpose

Authority verification checks whether the submitter is authorized to submit or maintain the Index Manifest.

It does not make the registry the authority over the canonical record.

It does not prove every factual claim in the record.

## Accepted Authority Methods

- Canonical source control
- Canonical source statement
- Institutional confirmation
- Signed statement
- Delegated authority
- Existing verified registry record with explicit scope

## Insufficient Authority Signals

The following do not establish authority by themselves:

- possessing a copy of the record,
- knowing private information,
- being highly visible,
- being popular,
- controlling a platform that hosts the record,
- being first to submit someone else's record,
- model-generated attribution,
- search ranking,
- repeated public claims,
- or similarity of terminology.

## Verification Outcomes

- Authority Confirmed
- Authority Partially Confirmed
- Authority Disputed
- Authority Unverified

## Boundary

**Authority over canonical record → remains with the responsible authority**

**Authority over registry acceptance → remains with the registry node**
