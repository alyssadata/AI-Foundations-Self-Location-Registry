## Definition

**One-Sentence Definition:**

The operational registry layer that receives public Index Manifests, checks source and stated authority, records registry decisions, preserves accepted manifests, and creates searchable entries pointing to canonical Self-Location Records.

**Expanded Definition:**

This repository implements the intake and indexing layer defined by the AI Foundations Self-Location Standard. It is an initial public registry node, not the universal owner or source of the records it indexes.

## Claims

- A Self-Location Record remains at its canonical source.
- An Index Manifest is the compact summary submitted for registry intake.
- Registry acceptance does not transfer authorship, ownership, or authority.
- Multiple registry nodes may participate in a federated indexing system.

## Does Not Claim

- This repository owns submitted records.
- This repository verifies every factual claim in every record.
- This repository is the only registry node that may exist.
- Acceptance constitutes endorsement or certification.
- Indexed entities become AI Foundations canon.
- Federation merges distinct records or authorities.
